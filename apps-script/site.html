ABOUT THE GAME

A first-person horror game. Survive five nights in the old house at 14 Marrow Lane. Seth is somewhere in the house, and he can hear you. Hide, stay quiet and make it to morning.

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D
Move
MOUSE
Look around
E / CLICK
Open, close, read, take, hide
SHIFT
Run (running is loud)
C
Crouch
F
Torch on or off
Q
Throw a bottle
1 – 5
Take out the torch or a gun, and put it away again
CLICK / SPACE
Fire, when a gun is out
R
Reload
ESC / P
Pause

On a phone or tablet: move with your left thumb, swipe with your right thumb to look, and use the buttons on screen for everything else.

Watch the eye at the top of the screen. It opens when Seth is near, and if it turns red, he has found you.

WORKS ON

  • Computer, with keyboard and mouse
  • Tablet
  • Phone

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Best played in the dark, with headphones.
  • Your progress is saved in your browser.
  • Graphics can be set to Full or Lighter in the game's settings.

WARNING

  • Contains jump scares, flashing lights and loud sounds.
