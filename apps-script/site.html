HORRORFIRST-PERSONSINGLE PLAYER

ABOUT THE GAME

A first-person horror game. Survive five nights in the old house at 14 Marrow Lane. Seth is somewhere in the house, and he can hear you. Hide, stay quiet and make it to morning.

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D
Move
MOUSE
Look around
E / CLICK
Open, close, read, take, hide
SHIFT
Run (running is loud)
C
Crouch
F
Torch on or off
Q
Throw a bottle
1 – 5
Take out the torch or a gun, and put it away again
CLICK / SPACE
Fire, when a gun is out
R
Reload
ESC / P
Pause

On a phone or tablet: move with your left thumb, swipe with your right thumb to look, and use the buttons on screen for everything else.

Watch the eye at the top of the screen. It opens when Seth is near, and if it turns red, he has found you.

WORKS ON

  • Computer, with keyboard and mouse
  • Tablet
  • Phone

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Best played in the dark, with headphones.
  • Your progress is saved in your browser.
  • Graphics can be set to Full or Lighter in the game's settings.

WARNING

  • Contains jump scares, flashing lights and loud sounds.

RACINGTRICKSONLINE MULTIPLAYER

ABOUT THE GAME

Send a dirt bike down a huge mountain at sunset. Hit the jumps, throw Supermans, Can-Cans and flips, race the clock, smash through the course, or host a game and ride with a mate online. Crash hard and you are TAKEN OUT.

HOW TO PLAY

W / S
Gas and brake
A D
Steer (in the air: spin)
SHIFT / CTRL
Lean back (wheelie) / lean forward
S / W in the air
Backflip / front flip
F + direction
Tricks: Superman, No-Hander, Can-Can, Nac-Nac
X
Back brake, to slide round corners
SPACE
Hop
R
Respawn
ESC
Pause

On a phone or tablet: steer with the stick on the left, and use the buttons on the right for gas, brake and tricks.

WORKS ON

  • Computer, with keyboard or a controller
  • Tablet
  • Phone

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Multiplayer: one of you hosts and sends the join code, the other types it in.
  • Your level and XP are saved in your browser.

WARNING

  • Contains cartoon crashes with blood and gore.
